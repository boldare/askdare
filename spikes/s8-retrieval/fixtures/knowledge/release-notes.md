# Release notes

## October 2026

- Dark mode for the mobile apps.
- Automations can now post to Slack.

## March 2026

- New prices: the Team plan goes from €6 to €8 per user per month for new customers. Existing Team customers keep €6 until March 2027.
- Storage on the Team plan grows from 50 GB to 100 GB.

## November 2025

- Timeline view leaves beta.
- Guests can now be added to the Team plan, not only to Business.
