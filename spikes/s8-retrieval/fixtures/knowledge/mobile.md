# Mobile apps

Brightloop has native apps for iOS (iOS 17 or later) and Android (Android 11 or later). They are free on every plan.

## Offline mode

The apps keep the projects you opened in the last 7 days available offline. Changes made offline sync when the device reconnects; if two people changed the same field, the latest change wins and the other one is kept in the task's history.

## Notifications

Push notifications can be limited to mentions and assigned tasks in **Profile → Notifications**. Quiet hours mute all push notifications between two times you choose.

## Limits

Timeline view and the audit log are not available in the mobile apps; use the web app for them.
