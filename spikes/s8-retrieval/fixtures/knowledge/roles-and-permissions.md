# Roles and permissions

Every person in a workspace has one of four roles.

| Role   | Can do                                                                 |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner  | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace               |
| Admin  | Manage members, integrations and project settings; no billing          |
| Member | Create and edit projects and tasks they have access to                 |
| Guest  | See and comment on the projects they are invited to; cannot edit tasks |

A workspace can have more than one owner. The last owner cannot leave the workspace until they make someone else an owner.

## Private projects

Members create private projects that only invited people can see. Admins can see that a private project exists, but not its tasks, unless they are invited.

## Guests

Guests are free and do not take a seat (see pricing). Guest access is available on Team and Business. Each guest needs an email address; guests cannot be invited by a link.
