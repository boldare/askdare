@AGENTS.md

This is the chatdock starter app: the e2e target, the scaffold source and the docs sample. Keep it minimal and in line with the root `CLAUDE.md`. The home page doubles as the Boldare-branded demo: one bot per theme (`DEMO_BOTS`), switched with `?theme=` and `?mode=bubble|inline`, the chat script added after hydration (`app/chat-script.tsx`), a three.js shape per theme in the hero (`app/_shape/`, loaded on demand: a sphere, cone, cylinder or cube as liquid or a network, switched with `?look=`), and BLDR as the custom-theme example (`defineTheme` + `widget.css` in `chatdock.config.ts`). `AGENTS.md` is generated by `next dev` (Next.js 16.3+); commit it when it changes.
