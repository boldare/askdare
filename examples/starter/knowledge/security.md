# Security and privacy

## Where data is stored

All customer data is stored in the European Union, in data centres in Frankfurt (primary) and Amsterdam (backup). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

## Backups

Databases are backed up every day and kept for 35 days. Backups are tested by a monthly restore drill.

## Access and sign-in

- Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and can be enforced by workspace owners on Team and Business.
- Single sign-on with SAML (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace) is available on the Business plan.
- The audit log on the Business plan keeps one year of sign-ins, permission changes and exports.

## Compliance

- Brightloop signs a GDPR data processing agreement (DPA) with every paying customer on request.
- An independent ISO/IEC 27001 audit takes place every year.
- Security issues can be reported to security@example.com; we reply within 2 business days.
