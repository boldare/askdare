# Getting help

The Brightloop support team works Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 Central European Time, except Polish public holidays.

## How to reach us

- In the app: **Help → Contact support** (fastest, includes your workspace details).
- Email: support@example.com.
- Status of the service and incidents: https://example.com/status.

## Response times

| Plan     | First response            |
| -------- | ------------------------- |
| Free     | community forum only      |
| Team     | within 1 business day     |
| Business | within 4 business hours   |

Business customers also get a named customer success manager and an onboarding call.

## Importing data

Brightloop imports projects from CSV files, Trello and Asana. Go to **Settings → Import**. Attachments larger than 100 MB are skipped and listed in the import report.

## Deleting a workspace

Workspace owners can delete a workspace in **Settings → Workspace → Delete**. Data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently deleted.
