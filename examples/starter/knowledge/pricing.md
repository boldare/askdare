# Pricing and plans

Brightloop is a fictional project management app used to demo chatdock. All prices are in euros and exclude VAT.

| Plan     | Price                    | Users        | Highlights                                             |
| -------- | ------------------------ | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free     | €0                       | up to 3      | Unlimited projects, 1 GB storage, board and list views |
| Team     | €8 per user per month    | up to 50     | Timeline view, guest access, 100 GB storage            |
| Business | €15 per user per month   | unlimited    | SSO (SAML), audit log, 1 TB storage, priority support  |

## Billing

- Paying yearly saves 20% on Team and Business.
- Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial; no card is needed to start it.
- Seats are billed per active user. Guests are free and do not take a seat.
- Invoices are sent by email on the first day of each billing period.

## Changing or cancelling a plan

- Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated.
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
- You can cancel at any time in **Settings → Billing**. Monthly plans are not refunded; yearly plans cancelled within 30 days of payment are refunded in full.

## Discounts

Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off the Business plan. Apply through the support team with proof of status.
