Adds admin (ADR-0016, ADR-0032): an `AdminStore` for usage, ratings and transcripts, the `admin` option of `createChatHandler`, and a console at `<basePath>/admin`. With `@boldare/askdare-admin` the console also shows a dashboard.

- **The `AdminStore` port** holds per-turn usage, ratings, optional transcripts, and daily counts of handoffs and leads (counts only, never their values), with fixed queries bounded by a day range of at most 400 days and pages of at most 100. Use `postgresAdminStore()` from `@boldare/askdare-postgres` or `upstashAdminStore()` from `@boldare/askdare-upstash`; `memoryAdminStore()` keeps records in memory for tests and warns in production. Store authors run `adminStoreContract` from `@boldare/askdare/testing`.
- **`admin.store`:** askdare writes each turn's counters, every rating and, with `admin.transcripts: true`, each turn's text to the store itself, after the response and next to your hooks. A failed write goes to `onError` by its error's class name and never fails the turn. Answers can be rated with an admin store even without `onFeedback`. `admin.retentionDays` (1 to 400, default 30) is the one place retention is set: askdare passes it to the store with every write, so a store that expires records on its own never does it sooner.
- **Delete and sweep reach the store.** `DELETE /conversation` deletes the conversation's admin records first, then the conversation, then its files. The handlers now include `sweep({ idleDays?, limit?, signal? })`, which deletes idle conversations with their files and records, then records older than `admin.retentionDays`, and answers `{ conversations, files, records, more }`. Call it from your own cron route instead of deleting files with the `openai` SDK. `askdare doctor` warns when records older than the retention remain.
- **`admin.console`:** a console at `<basePath>/admin`, off by default. Owners sign in with `ADMIN_SECRET` (at least 32 characters, different from `CHAT_SECRET` and `IDENTITY_SECRET`) or with an identity token whose `sub` is in `admin.console.allow` (audience `askdare:admin`, at most 10 minutes long, passed as `#identity=<jwt>`). Both are checked on the console's first request, not when the route module loads, so `next build` passes without the secret. The console shows each bot's resolved configuration from a fixed list of fields, runs `doctor`, has a playground whose turns count against the bot's limits and reach `onUsage` and the store marked `console`, lists stored conversations for review, and plugs in `admin.console.dashboard` (the `AdminDashboard` type) from `@boldare/askdare-admin`. Sessions last 15 minutes and live in memory only; sign-ins are limited to 5 a minute per IP and 20 an hour; the browser never gets a raw conversation ID. The console is loaded only when it is on, and costs the loader, the React entry and the chat app nothing.

The guide is `docs/admin.md`.
