Add themes. Four built-in presets, each in light and dark, chosen with `widget.theme`: **Halo** (the default; a frosted orb and a centred greeting), **Aura** (pastel glass and an iridescent orb), **Sage** (calm and editorial, with a persona card and pill buttons) and **Care** (hyperlegible type, AAA text contrast, and an opening message with a checklist). `defineTheme({ extends, accent, tokens, dark, variants })` builds your own on top of a preset; a brand `accent` gets readable text automatically, and a customized theme with weak contrast triggers a warning in development. New widget options: `subtitle`, `starters` (suggested questions), `avatar`, `launcherLabel` and `colorScheme`. The theme is served as a per-bot stylesheet, the fonts (OFL) are self-hosted and load only for the preset in use, the launcher on the host page follows the theme (orb, circle or pill), and `createChatHandler({ gallery: true })` serves a state gallery for design review and visual tests.
