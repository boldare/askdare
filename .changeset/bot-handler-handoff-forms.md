Turns on handoff to a person (ADR-0030) and forms with leads (ADR-0031) in bots and the chat route.

- **`defineBot({ handoff, forms })`.** `handoff: true` or `{ model, button, form, available, content, limits }`: the model may call the reserved `handoff` tool (a reason from a fixed list, and with `content: 'summary'` or `'transcript'` a short summary), and the visitor's "Talk to a person" calls the new `POST /handoff`. `forms` takes up to 10 forms made with `defineForm`; those the model may show add the reserved `show_form` tool. `handoff` and `show_form` are refused as names of the bot's own tools. The public configuration gets `forms` in the visitor's language, never a form's description, `model` or `toModel`.
- **`createChatHandler({ onHandoff, onLead })`.** `onHandoff` (for example `webhookHandoff()` or `emailHandoff()`) is awaited with a 10-second deadline and decides the state the visitor sees: `sent`, `saved`, `unavailable`, `failed` or `limited`. `onLead` gets each sent form's validated values after the response. The handler refuses to start when a bot turns handoff on without `onHandoff`, or offers forms to the model without `onLead`; `askdare doctor` confirms both.
- **`POST /forms`** validates a submission against the form's definition and answers `{ state: 'sent' }` (with `handoff` for a handoff's form), `400` with an error key per field and never a value, `409` once it was sent, `404` for a handle of another visitor, conversation or bot. Submissions are limited to 5 a minute and 20 a day per visitor and per IP.
- **Limits.** Handoffs default to 1 per conversation, 3 per visitor and per IP a day, and 100 per bot a day; `onError` hears once a day when a bot runs out. The `handoff` tool isn't offered once the conversation used its handoffs, and a turn hands off and shows a form once at most; neither tool is offered on a turn's last step.
- **Conversation and history.** A handoff from the button and every sent form leave a fixed developer note without content; fields marked `toModel` follow as a user message. `POST /history` gives answers their `handoff` state and `forms`, with a fresh handle for each open form.
- **No values in logs.** askdare stores and logs no form values; a failing `onHandoff`, `onLead` or `available` reaches `onError` by its error's class name only. With an admin store, handoffs are counted by final state and leads once handed to `onLead` (ADR-0032).
