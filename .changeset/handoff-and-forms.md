Adds handoff to a person (ADR-0030) and forms with leads (ADR-0031) to bots and the chat route. Handoff is one-way: askdare delivers the request to your webhook or mailbox, and a person answers the visitor by email.

- **`defineForm`** declares a form a bot can show in the chat, from a closed set of fields: `text`, `email`, `phone`, `select`, `textarea` and `consent`. Labels, titles and the optional `submit` and `done` take a string or a map by language. The form is checked at startup (names, at most 8 fields, lengths of texts and values, 2 to 10 options) and throws with every reason. Its `description` is for the model only, and `model: false` keeps a form for a handoff. Each field's `toModel` is off by default, so the model learns only that the form was sent.
- **`defineBot({ handoff, forms })`.** `handoff: true` or `{ model, button, form, available, content, limits }`: the model may call the reserved `handoff` tool (a reason from a fixed list and, with `content: 'summary'` or `'transcript'`, a short summary), and the visitor's "Talk to a person" button calls the new `POST /handoff`. When `available()` says nobody is there, the handoff's form takes a message instead. `forms` takes up to 10 forms; those the model may show add the reserved `show_form` tool. `handoff` and `show_form` are refused as names of the bot's own tools. The public configuration gets `forms` in the visitor's language and `handoff: true` when the bot shows the button, never a form's description, `model` or `toModel`, nor the rest of the handoff settings.
- **`createChatHandler({ onHandoff, onLead })`.** `onHandoff` is awaited with a 10-second deadline and decides the state the visitor sees: `sent`, `saved`, `unavailable`, `failed` or `limited`. `onLead` gets each sent form's validated values after the response; a message left through the handoff's form is awaited like `onHandoff`, since its outcome is what the visitor sees. The handler refuses to start when a bot turns handoff on without `onHandoff`, or offers forms to the model without `onLead`; `askdare doctor` confirms both.
- **Two transports for `onHandoff`** in `@boldare/askdare/next`:
  - `webhookHandoff({ url, secret, timeoutMs?, headers? })` posts the `ChatHandoffReport` as JSON, signed with Standard Webhooks headers (`webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, `webhook-signature`), so any Standard Webhooks verifier accepts it. It sends over https only (plain http just for localhost outside production), follows no redirects, gives each attempt 4 seconds by default and retries a network error, timeout, `408`, `429` or `5xx` once after 500 ms, within the handoff's ten seconds.
  - `emailHandoff({ to, from, send })` formats the report as a plain-text email and passes it to your own `send` function (Resend, Postmark, SES, nodemailer…). The visitor's email becomes `replyTo`; the subject holds only fixed text, the bot and the reason.

  Both throw when the request was not delivered, and their errors never quote the report or the receiver's answer.
- **`POST /forms`** validates a submission against the form's definition and answers `{ state: 'sent' }` (with `handoff` for a handoff's form), `400` with an error key per field and never a value, `409` once it was sent, `404` for a handle of another visitor, conversation or bot. Submissions are limited to 5 a minute and 20 a day per visitor and per IP.
- **Handoff limits.** By default 1 per conversation, 3 per visitor and per IP a day, and 100 per bot a day; `onError` hears once a day when a bot runs out. The `handoff` tool isn't offered once the conversation used its handoffs, a turn hands off and shows a form once at most, and neither tool is offered on a turn's last step.
- **The conversation and its history.** A handoff from the button and every sent form leave a fixed developer note without content; fields marked `toModel` follow as a user message. `POST /history` gives answers their `handoff` state and `forms`, with a fresh handle for each open form.
- **No values in logs.** askdare stores and logs no form values; a failing `onHandoff`, `onLead` or `available` reaches `onError` by its error's class name only. With an admin store, handoffs are counted by final state and leads once handed to `onLead` (ADR-0032).
- **The protocol** grows additively in version 1 ([protocol](https://askdare.vercel.app/docs/protocol#handoff)): the `handoff` and `form` events, `handoff` and `forms` on answers in `POST /history`, `POST /handoff` and `POST /forms`, and `forms` and `handoff` in a bot's public configuration. Clients from 0.6 ignore the new events and fields.

The guides are `docs/handoff.md` and `docs/forms.md`.
