Starting a new conversation now asks inside the chat, in the theme's style, instead of with the browser's own dialog. Focus starts on Cancel and stays in the question; Escape answers no without closing the chat. New text keys `confirm.cancel` and `confirm.accept`, and new parts `confirm-backdrop`, `confirm`, `confirm-icon`, `confirm-text`, `confirm-actions`, `confirm-cancel` and `confirm-accept`.
