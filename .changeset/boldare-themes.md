Aura, now in Boldare's colors, is the default theme: a glossy bubble with its reflection, a two-weight headline (`**bold**` words in a greeting), pill suggestions and a card composer with a Send pill (the new `send-label` part). Halo gains a ringed frosted orb, a bold question and a round field with a soft blue mic; Sage gains light type, a mint assistant bubble and the mic beside the field, and no longer loads a serif font. An inline chat whose script runs before its element exists now waits for the page instead of floating.
