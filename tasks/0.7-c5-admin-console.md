# c5. The admin console (stage 1), core writing to the `AdminStore`, delete and sweep

- **Status:** blocked (p2)
- **Release:** 0.7 (base branch `release/0.7`)
- **Lane:** Core
- **Touches hot files:** Yes (`server/handler.ts`)
- **Changes the public API or the protocol:** Yes: API snapshot and a minor changeset
- **Depends on:** p2. Not at the same time as c3

## Goal

An owner opens the console route, signs in with `ADMIN_SECRET` or an allowlisted identity, and sees each bot's resolved configuration (an allowlist of fields), runs `doctor` and tries the bot in a playground, as ADR-0016 stage 1 describes. With `admin.store` set, core writes the turn, rating and transcript records and the handoff and lead counts itself, and deleting a conversation or sweeping old ones also deletes its records there. That closes the "transcripts outlive a deleted conversation" gap.

## Files and area

- `next/create-chat-handler.ts`: the `admin` option (`store`, `transcripts`, `retentionDays` default 30, `console` with `allow` and `dashboard`); `ADMIN_SECRET` (at least 32 characters, different from `CHAT_SECRET` and `IDENTITY_SECRET`); `sweep({ idleDays?, limit?, signal? })` on the returned handlers, resolving to `{ conversations, files, records, more }`.
- `server/handler.ts`:
  - **writes:** the turn, rating and transcript records (transcripts only with `admin.transcripts`) and the handoff and lead counts, after the response next to the hooks; a failure goes to `onError` by class name; `POST /feedback` accepted when `onFeedback` or an admin store is set;
  - **delete:** after the ownership check, `admin.store.deleteConversation`, then `conversations.delete`, then the files;
  - **sweep:** `conversations.sweep` where the store has it, `deleteConversation` and `deleteFiles` per swept conversation, then `admin.store.sweep` with 30 days for records and 400 for daily rows;
  - **the console:** off by default (404 otherwise); `POST admin/api/session` exchanging the secret or an identity JWT (audience `askdare-admin`, at most 10 minutes) for a 15-minute admin token; sign-ins limited to 5 a minute per IP and 20 an hour across the app; the token checked on every request; sealed conversation references instead of raw IDs; the headers of decision 8; the dashboard plug-in served under the console.
- A new `server/admin-console.ts` and the console app in `src/admin-console/`, built and inlined like `widget-app/` (`scripts/inline-assets.mjs`) into its own generated module, loaded by dynamic `import()`, with a 30 KB gzipped budget. Never read from disk at runtime.
- `server/explain.ts`: reuse it for the resolved instructions in the playground.
- **The import-boundary check:** a lint rule or a test over the built entries proving that nothing under `embed/`, `react/`, `chat/` or `widget-app/` reaches `admin/` or `admin-console/`.
- `askdare doctor`: the admin store's `diagnose()`, with a warning when records older than the retention remain.
- `THREAT_MODEL.md`: finish the draft section (T35 to T38) and merge it into the table.
- `docs/own-your-data.md`: the cron route calls the handlers' `sweep()` instead of the `openai` SDK.

## Don't touch
