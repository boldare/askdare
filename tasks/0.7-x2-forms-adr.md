## Goal

ADR-0031 (Proposed) decides how a bot asks for structured data in the chat (a lead, a contact form, the form a handoff falls back to) and hands it to `onLead`, validated and without leaking it.

## What the ADR decides

- **Where forms are defined:** in server configuration only (`defineBot` or a `defineForm` helper), with fields from a small closed set (text, email, phone, select, checkbox for consent, textarea). The model picks a form by name; it never invents fields.
- **How it is shown:** as a `tool.ui` card rendered by the widget, through `toolRenderers`, or a new protocol event. Which one, and why.
- **How it is submitted:** a new endpoint or a message in the turn; server-side validation (zod), per-visitor limits, and the same-origin and token checks every endpoint has.
- **What the model sees after a submit:** "submitted" only, or the values. Default: nothing personal goes back to the model unless the form says so.
- **What `onLead` gets:** the form name, the validated values, pseudonym, identity if signed in, conversation ID, locale. Never logged; listed in `docs/privacy.md`.
- **Consent:** how a form shows a consent checkbox or a privacy link, and that askdare stores nothing itself.
