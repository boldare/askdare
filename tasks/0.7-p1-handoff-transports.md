## Goal

The transports ADR-0030 chooses exist and are tested on their own: a signed webhook with retries and a time limit, and email through the route ADR-0030 picks. c3 only has to call them.

## Decisions to read first

- ADR-0030 (x1), [ADR-0004](../docs/adr/0004-threat-model.md), [ADR-0012](../docs/adr/0012-server-hooks-and-telemetry.md) (hook errors go to `onError`, no content in logs).
- The `typescript-conventions` skill.

## Files and area

- `packages/askdare/src/handoff/`: the transport interface, `webhookHandoff()` (HMAC signature header, timeout, bounded retries, no redirects to other hosts) and the email transport.
- `packages/askdare/src/testing/`: a contract suite for transports, if ADR-0030 lets apps write their own.
- Exports from `src/index.ts` only as ADR-0030 lists. Regenerate the snapshot (`pnpm --filter @boldare/askdare api:update`).

## Don't touch

- `server/handler.ts`, `bot/define-bot.ts`, the protocol (c1, c3).
