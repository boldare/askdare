## Goal

An app can keep its own transcripts: an opt-in `onTranscript` hook gets the visitor's text and the answer after each turn, with the same identifiers `onFeedback` gets. It is what admin stage 2 (0.7) stores, and it works with every conversation store.

## Decisions to read first

- ADR-0016 (admin, stage 2: `onTranscript`, GDPR rules), ADR-0012 (hooks are isolated and never break a turn), ADR-0022 (what an answer is after output checks), ADR-0004 (no content in logs).

## Files and area

- `next/create-chat-handler.ts` and `server/handler.ts`: the option, documented next to `onUsage` and `onFeedback`.
- `server/stream-turn.ts`: call it after the turn ends, through `waitUntil` like the other hooks, with the text the visitor sent (after any redaction by `onInput`) and the answer the visitor saw (after output checks: the replacement for a replaced answer, nothing for a withheld one), its citations, the pseudonymous IDs and the end reason. Errors in the hook are caught and reported through `onError`, never logged with content.
- Docs: `docs/observability.md` or `docs/feedback.md` (wherever the other hooks live), `docs/privacy.md` (it is the only hook that carries content).
