# l2. Proactive greeting and an unread badge

- **Status:** blocked (l1, c4)
- **Release:** 0.7 (base branch `release/0.7`)
- **Lane:** Loader
- **Touches hot files:** Yes (`embed/loader.ts`, `bot/define-bot.ts` for the option, `protocol/launcher.ts` for the look)
- **Changes the public API or the protocol:** Yes: API snapshot and a minor changeset. The bridge protocol doesn't change.
- **Depends on:** l1, c4 (which adds the texts and is the last task before this one on `define-bot.ts`)

## Goal

A site can show a short greeting next to the closed launcher after a delay, and the launcher shows a badge when an answer arrives while the chat is closed, accessibly and within the loader's 5,632-byte budget (ADR-0033; the spike measured about 5,195 B).

## Decisions to read first

- ADR-0033 and `spikes/s10-loader/FINDINGS.md`, [ADR-0019](../docs/adr/0019-compliance-and-guardrails.md) (the AI notice reaches the greeting too), [ADR-0013](../docs/adr/0013-browser-events.md), [ADR-0014](../docs/adr/0014-runtime-language.md).
- `spikes/s10-loader/patches.mjs`: `greeting-bubble` (with the gate above it) and `badge-attr` are the measured and browser-checked versions. `demo/serve.mjs` runs them on a host page.

## What it builds

- **The greeting, drawn by the loader:** off by default; a bot option with a delay (name settled with z1; `widget.greeting` stays the welcome text). The look gets the delay and, only for a bot with a greeting, the `greeting` and `dismiss` texts per language.
  - Shows after the delay, only with askdare's launcher, not after the chat was opened, not if the session already dismissed it or opened the chat.
  - `data-greeting="off"` on the script tag turns it off for the page.
  - `sessionStorage` key `askdare:<bot>:greeted`, written only on dismiss or open; blocked storage means no greeting.
  - No focus steal, no live region; × (24 × 24 px) and Escape dismiss it and focus the launcher; reduced motion turns off its animation; `textContent` only.
  - Events `greeting.shown` and `greeting.dismissed`, emitted by the loader like `open` and `close`.
- **The badge:** counts `message.received` with `outcome: 'completed'` while the panel is closed; `data-unread` on the launcher, drawn by `::after`, `9+` above nine; cleared on open. The launcher's name gains the `unread` text. No new bridge message.
