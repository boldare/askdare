## What the ADR decides

- **Who triggers it:** a reserved `handoff` tool the model calls, a button the visitor presses, or both; and how a bot turns it on in `defineBot`.
- **What `onHandoff` gets:** reason, pseudonym, identity if signed in, conversation ID, and whether the transcript is included. Content only when the app opts in, as with `onTranscript`.
- **Transports in 0.7:** webhook (signed payload, retries, timeout) and email. For email, whether askdare sends through a function the app supplies or through a small SMTP or HTTP adapter, with no vendor SDK in the core.
- **What the visitor sees:** a "connecting you" state, a confirmation, and what happens when nobody answers or the transport fails (falls back to a form from x2, or a message).
- **Does the conversation continue** with the bot after handoff, or is it closed for the bot? One-way handoff (the human answers by email) in 0.7; live agent chat stays in 0.9 (Slack, HubSpot, Zendesk adapters).
- **Protocol:** the new SSE event or `tool.ui` payload, additive within v1 ([`docs/protocol.md`](../docs/protocol.md)).
- **Limits and abuse:** a handoff budget per visitor and per day, since each one reaches a person.
