## Goal

`pgvectorIndex({ url, embedder })` keeps a bot's knowledge in the site's Postgres, syncs incrementally and searches as well as S8's prototype did.

## Decisions to read first

- [ADR-0025](../docs/adr/0025-knowledge-index.md) (Proposed), decisions 1, 4, 7 and 9.
- `spikes/s8-retrieval/`: `pg-index.ts`, `test-offline.ts`, and `FINDINGS.md` questions 2, 3 and 6.

## Files and area

- `packages/askdare-postgres/src/knowledge/`: tables keyed by index ID; chunks with their hash, source ID, section, text and embedding; an HNSW index and a `simple` full-text index; hybrid search fused by reciprocal rank with a text weight of 0.3; `passage(sourceId)` as one indexed lookup; one transaction per document; the embedder ID stored with the index, a mismatch forcing a full re-embed on the next sync.
- `diagnose()`: `knowledge.pgvector`, `knowledge.synced`, `knowledge.embedder`, and whether the database is reachable.
- Its migration in `src/schema/` (a1's machinery), and one export line in `src/index.ts`.
- Whichever of a3 and k3 merges second adds an end-to-end test of `askdare kb sync` against the CI Postgres.
