## Goal

A team deploying askdare finds, in one page, what the law asks of a website chatbot and how askdare meets each point, with the decisions left to them clearly marked.

## Decisions to read first

ADR-0003 (data stays in OpenAI), ADR-0004 (threat model), `docs/privacy.md`, `docs/going-public.md`, `docs/deployment.md`.

## Files and area

New `docs/compliance.md`, linked from `docs/going-public.md` and the docs index. It covers:
- **AI Act art. 50(1):** the disclosure (task 2).
- **GDPR:** OpenAI's DPA, retention of conversations in OpenAI and how deletion works, what `safety_identifier` holds, the privacy notice.
- **Data residency:** what OpenAI's regional data residency covers today and what it costs (check the `openaiDeveloperDocs` MCP server), and what 0.6 will add.
- **Accessibility:** a link to the statement (task 6).
- **The resume token:** it sits in `localStorage` for 30 days. In inline or `Root` mode, an XSS on the host app can read it and the conversation. This trade-off is not written down anywhere today.
- **Hosts other than Vercel:** Docker, `next start` behind a proxy, OpenNext. Also an explicit "Edge runtimes won't work".

Legal dates and articles are checked against EUR-Lex and the standard against ETSI before publishing. The page says it is not legal advice.
