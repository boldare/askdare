# r3a. Resume on the server: event ids and `POST /resume`

- **Status:** blocked (3a, r1)
- **Release:** 0.6 (base branch `release/0.6`)
- **Lane:** Core
- **Touches hot files:** Yes (`protocol/*`, `docs/protocol.md`, `server/handler.ts`, `server/stream-turn.ts`)
- **Changes the public API or the protocol:** Yes: protocol, additive within v1; `createChatHandler` option (snapshot regenerated, minor changeset)
- **Depends on:** 3a (protocol files and `server/handler.ts`; it comes after t1), r1

## Goal

A client whose stream dropped can ask for the rest of the answer, and gets exactly the events it missed, then the live ones.

## Decisions to read first

- [ADR-0027](../docs/adr/0027-stream-resume.md) (Proposed), decisions 3, 5, 6 and 7; ADR-0004 (ownership), ADR-0012 (`waitUntil`).

## Files and area

- `server/stream-turn.ts`: every event goes through r1's batching writer into the log, with its sequence number; the SSE frame gets `id: <seq>`.
- `server/handler.ts`: `POST /resume` with `{ botId, resumeToken, messageId, after }`. The turn is looked up from the conversation in the resume token; another conversation's turn, or an expired one, answers `404 not_found`. Same-origin and rate limits as `POST /messages`.
- `next/create-chat-handler.ts`: `resume: { log }`, defaulting to `memoryTurnLog()`.
- Exports: `TurnEventLog`, `memoryTurnLog`, and the turn log suite in `@boldare/askdare/testing`.
- **Keeping the turn alive after the response closes:** verify on a Vercel preview (`after()` or `waitUntil`) and on Heroku. Deploying needs the maintainer's approval; ask before deploying. Record the result in ADR-0027 and `docs/deployment.md`.
- Docs: `docs/protocol.md`, `docs/privacy.md` (the log and its time to live), `docs/configuration.md`.

## Don't touch

- `chat/*` (3b, r3b), `packages/askdare-upstash/` (r2).
