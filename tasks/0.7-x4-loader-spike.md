# x4. Spike S10 and ADR-0033: room in the loader for a greeting

- **Status:** ready
- **Release:** 0.7 (base branch `release/0.7`)
- **Lane:** Spikes
- **Touches hot files:** No (the spike lives in `spikes/s10-loader/`)
- **Changes the public API or the protocol:** No
- **Depends on:** none

## Goal

We know how to fit a proactive greeting and an unread badge into the loader's 5,120-byte budget (gzipped; 5,016 B today), and ADR-0033 (Proposed) records the choice: make the loader smaller, move logic into the iframe, or raise the budget, with numbers.

## Decisions to read first

- [ADR-0005](../docs/adr/0005-embedding-model.md), [ADR-0013](../docs/adr/0013-browser-events.md), [ADR-0018](../docs/adr/0018-embed-path.md).
- `packages/askdare/src/embed/loader.ts` and `scripts/inline-assets.mjs` (the budget check).
- WCAG 2.2 AA rules the greeting must keep ([ADR-0019](../docs/adr/0019-compliance-and-guardrails.md)): no focus steal, dismissible, respects reduced motion.

## What the spike measures

- How many bytes the loader can lose without changing behavior (dead code, shared helpers, shorter messages).
- The cost of a greeting bubble drawn by the loader vs. a tiny iframe loaded before the chat.
- How the unread count reaches the loader (the host bridge), and its cost.
- When the greeting shows (delay, per page, once per visitor without cookies: `sessionStorage` or nothing), and how a site turns it off.
